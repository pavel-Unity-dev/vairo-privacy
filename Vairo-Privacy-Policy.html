Privacy Policy for VAIRO

Effective date: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game VAIRO (the “Game”), developed and published by Vairo Studio, handles user information.

Information We Collect

VAIRO does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal or sensitive user data. The Game does not require account registration and does not include advertising, analytics, cloud saves, online leaderboards, or in-app purchases.

Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, and other gameplay information are stored only on the user’s device. This local data is not transmitted to Vairo Studio or to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The current version of VAIRO does not use third-party advertising, analytics, authentication, or data-collection services.

Data Sharing

VAIRO does not share or sell user data because the Game does not collect user data.

Data Security

Vairo Studio does not receive or maintain personal user data from the Game. Local game data remains on the user’s device and is protected by the security measures of the device and operating system.

Data Retention and Deletion

Vairo Studio does not retain user data on its servers. Users can delete locally stored game data at any time by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling the Game. Vairo Studio has no server-side copy of that data.

Children’s Privacy

VAIRO does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. If you believe that information has been provided to us outside the Game, please contact us so that we can address the request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game’s features or data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

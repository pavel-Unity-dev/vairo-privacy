Privacy Policy for VAIRO

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game VAIRO (the “Game”), developed and published by Vairo Studio, handles user information.

Information We Collect

Vairo Studio does not operate its own server for VAIRO and does not require users to create an account. Vairo Studio does not directly collect payment card or bank account details. However, the Game uses Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play Billing. These services may automatically process the technical and purchase-related information described below to provide purchases, maintain service reliability, and perform limited service analytics. The Game does not include advertising, cloud saves, or online leaderboards.

Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, virtual coin balance, and other gameplay information are stored only on the user’s device. This local data is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by Vairo Studio.

In-App Purchases

VAIRO offers optional consumable purchases of virtual coin packs through Google Play Billing. Available packs may include 500, 2,000, and 4,000 virtual coins. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

Vairo Studio does not receive or store the user’s full payment card or bank account information. To complete and deliver a purchase, the Game may receive purchase-related information from Google Play, such as the product identifier, purchase status, purchase token, and transaction or order identifier. This information is used only to validate and fulfill the purchase. Vairo Studio does not maintain a server-side copy of this information.

Unity In-App Purchasing

VAIRO uses Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP), provided by Unity Technologies, to connect the Game with Google Play Billing. Depending on the device, store response, and purchase activity, Unity IAP may automatically collect the following categories of data: approximate location, email address, user identifiers, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostic information, other application performance data, and device or other identifiers.

These data may be processed for application functionality and limited analytics, including completing purchases, diagnosing errors, maintaining performance, and improving the reliability of the purchasing service. Some data may be processed temporarily, while purchase, diagnostic, and identifier information may be retained according to Unity's applicable policies. Vairo Studio does not keep a separate copy of these data on its own server. For more information, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing

VAIRO uses Google Play Billing to process optional in-app purchases. Google may process transaction and device information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. The Game does not use third-party advertising, authentication, or cloud-save services.

Data Sharing

Vairo Studio does not sell personal data. The Game exchanges purchase-related and technical information with Unity IAP and Google Play only as necessary to provide purchases, protect the service, diagnose problems, and perform the limited analytics described in this Policy.

Data Security

Vairo Studio does not maintain its own server database of personal user data or purchase records. Local game data remains on the user’s device and is protected by the security measures of the device and operating system. Data transmitted to Unity and Google is protected in transit by the security measures used by those services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Vairo Studio does not retain user data on its servers. Users can delete locally stored game data at any time by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling the Game. This also removes locally stored progress and virtual coin balance. Vairo Studio has no server-side copy of that data. Information processed by Unity or Google in connection with purchases, diagnostics, identifiers, or service analytics is governed by the respective provider’s retention and deletion practices. Users may also contact Vairo Studio at the address below for assistance with a privacy request.

Children’s Privacy

VAIRO is intended for users aged 13 and older. Vairo Studio does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children. Unity and Google may process limited technical or purchase-related data as described above and under their own privacy policies. If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to Vairo Studio, please contact us so that we can address the request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game’s features or data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

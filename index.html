Privacy Policy for VAIRO

Effective date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game VAIRO (the “Game”), developed and published by Vairo Studio, handles user information.

Information We Collect

Vairo Studio does not operate a server for VAIRO and does not directly collect or store personal or sensitive user data. The Game does not require account registration and does not include advertising, analytics, cloud saves, or online leaderboards.

Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, virtual coin balance, and other gameplay information are stored only on the user’s device. This local data is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by Vairo Studio.

In-App Purchases

VAIRO offers optional consumable purchases of virtual coin packs through Google Play Billing. Available packs may include 500, 2,000, and 4,000 virtual coins. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

Vairo Studio does not receive or store the user’s full payment card or bank account information. To complete and deliver a purchase, the Game may receive purchase-related information from Google Play, such as the product identifier, purchase status, purchase token, and transaction or order identifier. This information is used only to validate and fulfill the purchase. Vairo Studio does not maintain a server-side copy of this information.

Third-Party Services

VAIRO uses Google Play Billing to process optional in-app purchases. Google may process transaction and device information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. The Game does not use third-party advertising, analytics, authentication, or cloud-save services.

Data Sharing

Vairo Studio does not sell user data. Purchase-related information is exchanged with Google Play only as necessary to process, validate, and fulfill optional in-app purchases.

Data Security

Vairo Studio does not maintain personal user data or purchase records on its own servers. Local game data remains on the user’s device and is protected by the security measures of the device and operating system. Payment processing is protected by Google Play’s security measures.

Data Retention and Deletion

Vairo Studio does not retain user data on its servers. Users can delete locally stored game data at any time by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling the Game. This also removes locally stored progress and virtual coin balance. Vairo Studio has no server-side copy of that data. Information retained by Google in connection with purchases is governed by Google’s own retention and deletion practices.

Children’s Privacy

VAIRO does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. If you believe that information has been provided to us outside the Game, please contact us so that we can address the request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game’s features or data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
